Sequence space and the limits of a similarity threshold

Where the phylogeny shows descent, this page shows distance: which types are separable by sequence identity and which are not. It begins with the calibration that matters most, the identities between the characterised enzymes themselves.

1 · Why no single identity cut-off defines a function

Each row is a pair of curated reference enzymes with their experimentally determined substrates. Pairs with different substrates reach the top of the identity range, and pairs sharing a substrate reach far down it, because substrate selection in this family is set by a handful of active-site residues rather than by overall sequence.

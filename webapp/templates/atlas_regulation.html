Operon start, promoter region and the regulator facing it

Transcription of a catabolic operon begins at its 5' end, and the promoter lies in the intergenic region immediately upstream. This page locates that region for every confirmed alpha subunit, measures it, and reports what sits on the other side of it.

What is measured and what is not. The database contains the protein sequences of neighbouring genes, their coordinates and their strands, but not the intergenic DNA, because 88.9 % of the source GenBank records are CON entries that declare a length and carry no sequence. So the promoter region is located and measured here, while the promoter sequence is not analysed: no −35 or −10 boxes, no operator repeats, no transcription start site. Everything below concerns genomic architecture, which is observable from the annotation, and stops short of regulatory mechanism, which is not.

Where this database disagrees with the literature

A database that only reproduces what is already published has not earned its keep. These are the places where what is measured here does not match a published claim — including {{ dis.by_status.get('overturned, and it was our own', 0) + dis.by_status.get('overturned in part, and it was our own', 0) }} claims this project made earlier and has had to withdraw. Every number below is read from the analysis file that produced it, not retyped here, so it cannot drift away from the page that shows the working.

A disagreement is not a refutation. Everything on this page is a measurement over sequences and genomic context. That kind of measurement can show that a pattern does not generalise; it cannot overturn an experiment. A residue can be functionally essential and almost invariant; two proteins can be adjacent in a genome without ever interacting. Each entry therefore carries an explicit statement of what its test does not show, and that line is as much the result as the headline is.

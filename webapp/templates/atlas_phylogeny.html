Not every variant has a tip, and the rule is two-ended. A variant with a single member is its own representative. A variant with {{ cover.min_members_for_medoid }} or more members contributes the medoid of its members. Variants in between, with two to four members, are skipped, because a medoid of so few sequences is not a stable choice.

The active site, and how much of it varies

Every page before this one reasons about sequence. This one puts the sequence into three dimensions: which residues actually line the pocket around the catalytic iron, and which of them change between the members of a type.

{% if site %}

1 · Telling the two irons apart

A Rieske oxygenase carries two iron sites and they must not be confused. The Rieske centre is an iron pair bridged by inorganic sulfur, held by two cysteines and two histidines; the catalytic centre is a single iron held by two histidines and a carboxylate, with no sulfur. They were separated by that geometry rather than by trusting the file's labels, in all {{ site.coverage.types_with_catalytic_iron_resolved }} types that have a structure.

The four types that need the search window are the OxoO, OMO, CARDO and CarAa group. The profile had always tolerated an odd residue at one catalytic column in those four; the crystals show why. 2 · Pockets for the types with no crystal, and whether they can be trusted

Most types have never been crystallised. Their structures were not folded here; AlphaFold has already modelled them, and each model was accepted only when its sequence matched the curated one character for character. A predicted model contains no metal, so the pocket cannot be found by asking what sits near the iron. It is placed instead at the centre of the catalytic triad, which alignment locates reliably.

4 · Are pocket residues more conserved than the rest of the protein?

The obvious answer is yes, and the obvious answer is a tautology. Membership of this database requires all four Rieske ligands and at least two of the three iron ligands, so those residues are invariant because an entry lacking them was never admitted. They sit inside the pocket, so including them measures the admission rule rather than biology.

The same ratio, split by how similar the two variants are

A conservation contrast can only be seen in a window. Compare two almost identical sequences and nothing varies anywhere, so pocket and background divergence are both near zero and their ratio is an unstable quotient of two small numbers. Compare two distant sequences and both measures have already saturated at the background mismatch rate, so the ratio is pushed to one no matter what the pocket does. Only in between is there enough variation to see a difference and not so much that it is swamped. The row above averages over all three regimes at once. Pairs that are similar overall but differ in the pocket are the interesting case, and they are rare here, for a reason that is methodological rather than biological: variants are created by splitting a type until each piece is internally homogeneous, so two different variants of one type are by construction seldom very similar. The sample removes the candidates before the comparison begins.

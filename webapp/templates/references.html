Every source this database rests on

This is the project's citation registry, published whole. Each entry carries a DOI that was checked against Crossref, PubMed or the first page of the paper itself, and each one says what it is cited for — so a statement on this site can be followed back to the work it rests on. Nothing here was written from memory: an unverified identifier is worse than no identifier, because a reader who follows it and finds nothing will look for the mistake in themselves.

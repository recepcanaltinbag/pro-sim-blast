Rieske oxygenases: one catalytic architecture, many chemistries

A Rieske oxygenase alpha subunit carries two metal centres in one chain. A Rieske [2Fe-2S] cluster at the N-terminus accepts electrons from a ferredoxin, passes them across the subunit interface, and a mononuclear Fe(II) site at the C-terminus uses them to activate O2 and attack the substrate. Every one of the {{ totals.confirmed|fmt }} proteins in this database was confirmed to have both centres intact, so what differs between them is not the machinery but what they point it at.

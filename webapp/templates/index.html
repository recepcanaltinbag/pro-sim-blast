Rieske oxygenase alpha subunits, with their genomic neighbourhoods

Every entry is a protein found in a GenBank record that passed two independent tests: profile-HMM coverage of a full-length RO alpha model and the presence of the Rieske [2Fe-2S] ligands plus the mononuclear-iron catalytic triad at the aligned positions. For each one the ±10 kb neighbourhood is stored, operon structure is inferred from neighbour protein sequences (Pfam models for beta subunit, ferredoxin and reductase), and the entry is assigned to one of the reference RO types and to a sequence-homogeneous variant.

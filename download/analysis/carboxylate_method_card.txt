STEP 8  what the residue-distance spread means, and does not
------------------------------------------------------------
  median 8 residues, 95% interval -68..67; within +-10: 1036 of 2356
  reading     the register test is the ALIGNMENT column in step 6,
              where offset 0 means the homologous position. This
              raw distance measures something else: how much indel

              register, and why the anchor has to be per type.

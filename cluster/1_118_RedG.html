RedG · type 1_118_RedG · RO group 1

undecylprodigiosin natural_specialized literature confidence: high
This substrate was determined experimentally for the curated reference enzyme of this type. It is the compound that enzyme was tested on, not the limit of what it accepts: one Rieske oxygenase commonly turns over several related compounds, and most have been assayed against only a few. Of the 64 members assigned here, 20.3% are at least 60 % identical to that reference, so the label is informative for them; the rest are more distant relatives whose function is unknown.
Prodiginine alkaloid of Streptomyces. RedG carbocyclises an alkyl-chain carbon onto the ring to give streptorubin B; C-C bond formation rather than oxygen insertion into a product.
Curated from: UniProt O54095 (gene redG, SCO5897), Streptomyces coelicolor A3(2); proposed as a new type by analysis_out/literature_harvest.json finding NT-02, primary work Sydor PK et al., Nat Chem 2011, 3, 388-392 · from a published paper

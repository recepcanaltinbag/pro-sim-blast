PAO · type 6_601_PAO · RO group 6

pheophorbide a natural_specialized literature confidence: high
This substrate was determined experimentally for the curated reference enzyme of this type. It is the compound that enzyme was tested on, not the limit of what it accepts: one Rieske oxygenase commonly turns over several related compounds, and most have been assayed against only a few. Of the 339 members assigned here, 20.4% are at least 60 % identical to that reference, so the label is informative for them; the rest are more distant relatives whose function is unknown.
Chlorophyll catabolite in senescing plant plastids. Opening the chlorin macrocycle is the committed step of chlorophyll breakdown and is required to avoid phototoxic intermediates. Plastid enzyme; aligns to this set only over the Rieske domain.
Curated from: UniProt Q9FYC2 (PAO_ARATH, reviewed), Arabidopsis thaliana pheophorbide a oxygenase / ACD1 / LLS1 homolog, EC 1.14.15.17 Islevsel karakterizasyon: Pruzinska et al., Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A 2003;100:15259-15264, doi:10.1073/pnas.2036571100 (PAO'nun Rieske-tipi demir-sulfur proteini oldugunu gosteren makale) · no individual citation was recorded for this type

DAF36 · type 3_322_DAF36 · RO group 3

cholesterol natural_specialized literature confidence: high
This substrate was determined experimentally for the curated reference enzyme of this type. It is the compound that enzyme was tested on, not the limit of what it accepts: one Rieske oxygenase commonly turns over several related compounds, and most have been assayed against only a few. Of the 319 members assigned here, 1.3% are at least 60 % identical to that reference, so the label is informative for them; the rest are more distant relatives whose function is unknown.
The same C7-C8 desaturation as Neverland, in Caenorhabditis elegans, where it commits cholesterol to dafachronic acid and so to the dauer-formation decision.

PrnD · type 1_117_PrnD · RO group 1

aminopyrrolnitrin natural_specialized literature confidence: high
This substrate was determined experimentally for the curated reference enzyme of this type. It is the compound that enzyme was tested on, not the limit of what it accepts: one Rieske oxygenase commonly turns over several related compounds, and most have been assayed against only a few. Of the 160 members assigned here, 41.2% are at least 60 % identical to that reference, so the label is informative for them; the rest are more distant relatives whose function is unknown.
Intermediate of pyrrolnitrin biosynthesis in Pseudomonas. The six-electron aryl-amine to aryl-nitro oxidation is the final tailoring step of a bacterial antifungal, so this is a biosynthetic enzyme, not a degradative one.
Curated from: UniProt P95483 (PRND_PSEFL, reviewed), Pseudomonas fluorescens aminopyrrolnitrin oxygenase PrnD; proposed as a new type by analysis_out/literature_harvest.json finding NT-01 (Runda 2024 thesis, doi:10.33612/diss.1175898687), primary work Lee J, Simurdiak M, Zhao H, J Biol Chem 2005, 280, 36719-36728 · from a published paper

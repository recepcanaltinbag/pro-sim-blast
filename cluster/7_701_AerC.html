Biosynthetic intermediate of the aeruginosins, cyanobacterial peptides whose protease-inhibitory activity depends on the unusual arginine derivative Aeap. AerC builds the pyrroline ring of Aeap from the prenylated agmatine made by Aer3, so this is a biosynthetic enzyme in a secondary metabolite pathway, not a degradative one. The set reaches cyanobacteria otherwise only through SxtT and GxtA.

Most members of this type are closer to a different reference enzyme. Membership is decided by profile score, while "nearest reference" is measured by sequence identity, and the two are different questions. Here they disagree: of 26 members, only 15 (58 %) are in fact most similar to this type's own reference, and the average identity to the nearest reference is 49.4 %. At that identity no profile can separate the neighbouring types reliably, so read this as a region of sequence space rather than as a set of proteins like the reference.

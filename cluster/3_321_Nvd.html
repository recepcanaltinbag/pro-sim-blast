Nvd · type 3_321_Nvd · RO group 3

cholesterol natural_specialized literature confidence: high
This substrate was determined experimentally for the curated reference enzyme of this type. It is the compound that enzyme was tested on, not the limit of what it accepts: one Rieske oxygenase commonly turns over several related compounds, and most have been assayed against only a few. Of the 273 members assigned here, 4.8% are at least 60 % identical to that reference, so the label is informative for them; the rest are more distant relatives whose function is unknown.
Insect host sterol. Neverland performs the C7-C8 desaturation that commits cholesterol to ecdysteroid (moulting hormone) biosynthesis. An animal biosynthetic enzyme; it carries no beta subunit and does not degrade a pollutant.
Curated from: UniProt Q1JUZ1 (NVD_DROME, reviewed), Drosophila melanogaster cholesterol 7-desaturase Neverland, EC 1.14.19.21; proposed independently as a new type by analysis_out/literature_harvest.json finding NT-09 (Runda 2024 thesis, doi:10.33612/diss.1175898687) · no individual citation was recorded for this type

GdmA · type 1_116_GdmA · RO group 1

guaiacol (2-methoxyphenol) natural_aromatic literature confidence: high
This substrate was determined experimentally for the curated reference enzyme of this type. It is the compound that enzyme was tested on, not the limit of what it accepts: one Rieske oxygenase commonly turns over several related compounds, and most have been assayed against only a few. Of the 57 members assigned here, 43.9% are at least 60 % identical to that reference, so the label is informative for them; the rest are more distant relatives whose function is unknown.
Lignin-derived methoxyphenol. O-demethylation releases methanol and catechol and is a common entry step into bacterial catabolism of lignin monomers.
Curated from: PDB 7QWT, Rieske non-heme iron monooxygenase for guaiacol O-demethylation, Rhizorhabdus wittichii RW1; doi:10.1016/j.checat.2022.04.019; listed as unmapped in this repo's reference_structures.csv (best existing reference 1_107_CndA at 34.5%) · from a paper and a deposited structure

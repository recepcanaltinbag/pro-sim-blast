PTC52 · type 6_603_PTC52 · RO group 6

protochlorophyllide a natural_specialized literature confidence: medium
This substrate was determined experimentally for the curated reference enzyme of this type. It is the compound that enzyme was tested on, not the limit of what it accepts: one Rieske oxygenase commonly turns over several related compounds, and most have been assayed against only a few. Of the 140 members assigned here, 37.1% are at least 60 % identical to that reference, so the label is informative for them; the rest are more distant relatives whose function is unknown.
Plastid inner-envelope enzyme, proposed to oxidise the C7 methyl of protochlorophyllide and to be coupled to protein import across the envelope. Its physiological role is less firmly established than that of PAO or CAO, hence medium confidence.
Curated from: UniProt Q8W496 (PTC52_ARATH, reviewed), Arabidopsis thaliana protochlorophyllide-dependent translocon component 52 / ACD1-like / TIC55-IV, EC 1.14.15.- Islevsel karakterizasyon: Bartsch et al., Proc Natl Acad Sci U S A 2008;105:4933-4938, doi:10.1073/pnas.0800378105 · no individual citation was recorded for this type · curation confidence: medium

cadA · type 2_215_cadA · RO group 2

2,4-dichlorophenoxyacetate (2,4-D) xenobiotic literature confidence: low
This substrate was determined experimentally for the curated reference enzyme of this type. It is the compound that enzyme was tested on, not the limit of what it accepts: one Rieske oxygenase commonly turns over several related compounds, and most have been assayed against only a few. Of the 8 members assigned here, 75.0% are at least 60 % identical to that reference, so the label is informative for them; the rest are more distant relatives whose function is unknown.
Phenoxyacetate herbicide oxygenase of Bradyrhizobium sp. HW13. TENTATIVE: the source work discusses both 2,4-D and 2,4,5-T activity, so the preferred substrate is unsettled.

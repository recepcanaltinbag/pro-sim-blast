OxoO · type 1_101_OxoO · RO group 1

2-oxo-1,2-dihydroquinoline natural_aromatic literature confidence: medium
This substrate was determined experimentally for the curated reference enzyme of this type. It is the compound that enzyme was tested on, not the limit of what it accepts: one Rieske oxygenase commonly turns over several related compounds, and most have been assayed against only a few. Of the 36 members assigned here, 47.2% are at least 60 % identical to that reference, so the label is informative for them; the rest are more distant relatives whose function is unknown.
N-heterocyclic aromatic. Quinoline occurs both as a precursor of plant alkaloids and as a coal-tar component, so this class assignment is not clear-cut.

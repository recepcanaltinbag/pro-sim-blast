CAO · type 6_602_CAO · RO group 6

chlorophyllide a natural_specialized literature confidence: high
This substrate was determined experimentally for the curated reference enzyme of this type. It is the compound that enzyme was tested on, not the limit of what it accepts: one Rieske oxygenase commonly turns over several related compounds, and most have been assayed against only a few. Of the 138 members assigned here, 76.8% are at least 60 % identical to that reference, so the label is informative for them; the rest are more distant relatives whose function is unknown.
Plastid enzyme of chlorophyll b biosynthesis. Two successive hydroxylations turn the C7 methyl into a formyl group, which is exactly what distinguishes chlorophyll b from chlorophyll a.
Curated from: UniProt Q9MBA1 (CAO_ARATH, reviewed), Arabidopsis thaliana chlorophyllide a oxygenase / chlorophyll b synthase, EC 1.14.13.122 Islevsel karakterizasyon: Oster et al., Plant J 2000;21:305-310, doi:10.1046/j.1365-313x.2000.00672.x · no individual citation was recorded for this type

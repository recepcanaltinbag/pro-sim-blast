PsbAb · type 4_402_PsbAb · RO group 4

4-sulfobenzoate xenobiotic literature confidence: high
This substrate was determined experimentally for the curated reference enzyme of this type. It is the compound that enzyme was tested on, not the limit of what it accepts: one Rieske oxygenase commonly turns over several related compounds, and most have been assayed against only a few. Of the 28 members assigned here, 42.9% are at least 60 % identical to that reference, so the label is informative for them; the rest are more distant relatives whose function is unknown.
4-sulfobenzoate, a sulfonated aromatic of industrial origin; released sulfite enters sulfur metabolism.

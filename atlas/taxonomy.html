Which organisms carry which enzyme

Every confirmed alpha subunit is placed in the taxonomy of the genome record that contains it. Reading the composition of a type together with the number of distinct genera separates two histories: an enzyme inherited vertically within one lineage, and an enzyme that has moved between distant hosts.

3 · Phylum composition of every type

Eukaryotic Rieske oxygenases are not a contamination artefact: chloroplast enzymes such as choline monooxygenase, fungal and animal proteins carry an intact Rieske centre and catalytic triad and pass exactly the same test as the bacterial enzymes. Types with a substantial eukaryotic share are therefore excluded from the bacterial mobility statistics rather than silently mixed in.

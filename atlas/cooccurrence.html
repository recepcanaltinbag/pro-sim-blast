Enzymes that occur together

Testing the operon rule turned up something worth following: 854 times, a gene within 10 kb of an alpha subunit is itself a confirmed Rieske oxygenase. That can mean two different things, and separating them says something about how these pathways are built.

2 · Which pairs sit next to each other

Strand matters here. Two oxygenases on the same strand with a short gap can belong to one transcription unit. Two on opposite strands share a genome and a neighbourhood but are transcribed separately, so their association is about gene content rather than regulation.

3 · Which types share a genome more often than chance allows

Co-residence on its own is not informative, because two common enzymes will share a genome often simply by being common. 1,203 of 9,308 replicons carry more than one type of Rieske oxygenase, so multi-capability genomes are a minority but not a rarity.

Enzymes that occur together

Testing the operon rule turned up something worth following: 1,689 times, a gene within 10 kb of an alpha subunit is itself a confirmed Rieske oxygenase. That can mean two different things, and separating them says something about how these pathways are built.

2 · Which pairs sit next to each other

Strand matters here. Two oxygenases on the same strand with a short gap can belong to one transcription unit. Two on opposite strands share a genome and a neighbourhood but are transcribed separately, so their association is about gene content rather than regulation.

3 · Which types share a genome more often than chance allows

Co-residence on its own is not informative, because two common enzymes will share a genome often simply by being common. The test below shuffles type labels across all 12,329 replicons that carry an oxygenase, 2,000 times, while holding two things fixed: how many oxygenases each replicon has, and how many members each type has in total. Enrichment above that null is therefore not explained by abundance.

2,952 of 12,329 replicons carry more than one type of Rieske oxygenase, so multi-capability genomes are a minority but not a rarity.

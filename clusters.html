Reference RO types

Each confirmed entry is assigned to the reference type whose profile scores highest, by bit score rather than E-value. One row is one characterised enzyme type.

Click any column header to sort it; click again to reverse. The filter box matches anything in a row, and the chips narrow the table to one RO group, substrate class or literature confidence. Percentages are shares of that type's own members, so they are comparable down a column but not additive. A type with a low β, ferredoxin or reductase share is one whose partners are usually encoded elsewhere in the genome, not one that works without them. 10 of the 71 curated types have no members of their own; they are listed here with a no members mark and the type their reference is counted under, because assignment is competitive and a profile with a near-identical sibling can win nothing at all.

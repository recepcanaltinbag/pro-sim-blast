Rieske oxygenases: one iron architecture, many chemistries

Rieske oxygenases use O2 to do oxidations that are otherwise chemically hard. One alpha subunit chain holds both halves of the machine: a Rieske [2Fe-2S] cluster that delivers the electrons, and a mononuclear Fe(II) site that does the chemistry.

Two metal centres, twelve different oxidations

12distinct reactions
performed by 69 characterised enzyme types

Adding two hydroxyls across a ring at once, hydroxylating an unactivated carbon, stripping methyl groups from nitrogen or oxygen, desaturating, and cleaving rings open — all from the same pair of metal centres.

How this is counted

Distinct reaction classes among the curated enzyme types. The class of each type comes from the literature on its reference enzyme; type and entry counts are measured from the database on every build.

From aromatic pollutants to chlorophyll

12chemical families

The same fold is pointed at polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons, chlorinated and nitroaromatics, biphenyls and dioxin-like ethers, phthalate plasticisers, sulfonated aromatics, anilines, alkaloids such as caffeine, steroid and resin acid skeletons, quaternary-amine osmolytes and carnitine, and chlorophyll in senescing leaves.

How this is counted

Curated substrate families across the characterised types. The family assignment is a curator decision recorded in chemistry.csv; counts are measured from the database.

Bacteria, archaea and eukaryotes alike

3domains of life
35 phyla, 12,329 genome records

These are not a bacterial speciality. The architecture is present in plants, algae and fungi as well as across the bacterial tree, which is why a catalogue of it cannot be built from one clade's genomes.

How this is counted

Domain and phylum read from the taxonomy of each entry's source replicon, counted from the database. Entries without a genome record carry no taxonomy and are not counted here.

Measured over 21,046 entries. The Rieske centre is invariant across the whole database.

One of these columns does not read what it claims to read. Electrons cross the subunit interface, so the two carboxylates of this fold are different sites: one coordinates the iron, one relays electrons to a neighbour. Tested against 27 curated crystal structures, the bridging column passes — the residue it calls sits 5.9–7.2 Å from the neighbour's Rieske cluster. The catalytic column fails: the residue it calls is 10.9–14.7 Å from the iron in 26 of 26 testable structures, so no member counts are published for it. That is why membership requires two of three iron ligands rather than all three. The measurement, the structures and the open question →

2 · What the reaction actually is

The two metal centres support more than one chemistry. Counting types and members by reaction shows that the cis-dihydroxylation the family is named for is only the most common case, not the whole story.
